## Purpose and limits

This inventory makes the product's declared DPDP Act 2023 and CERT-In Cyber Security Directions (April 2022) workflow scope reviewable. It distinguishes data the current source model represents from statutory, operational, and deployment evidence that is not implemented or not verified. A field in an application schema does not by itself establish the lawful purpose, collection notice, statutory applicability, required retention, or completion of a duty.

Qualified counsel and the accountable security/operations owners must validate applicable provisions, amendments, rules, directions, sector-specific requirements, controller/data-fiduciary and processor roles, incident thresholds, reporting deadlines, retention periods, and territorial scope before this document is approved.

## Required processing and responsibility register

Complete this register for each processing activity and organization/service configuration before the legal/privacy gate is closed:

- Data-fiduciary/data-controller and data-processor role for each activity, including any joint or independent role.
- Processing activity, data-principal categories, personal-data fields, purpose, source, recipients, processors/subprocessors, and transfer locations.
- Applicable notice version/language, legal basis, consent/withdrawal behavior, non-consent path where applicable, children-related controls if applicable, and rights/grievance handling.
- Data-flow diagram covering application, endpoint, logs, webhook recipients, support access, backups, analytics, CI/CD artifacts, and incident response.
- Retention schedule by record and copy, including trigger/start event, exceptions/legal holds, deletion/anonymization method, downstream verification, and evidence owner.
- Security safeguards, access roles, encryption and key-management ownership, incident escalation, and breach-response decision authority.
- Legal review basis: applicable law/direction version, applicability rationale, jurisdiction, scope, counsel/reviewer identity and authority, review date, conditions, and signed record.

## CERT-In incident and log-evidence register

The security and legal owners must confirm applicability and operational interpretation. For each in-scope system and incident type, document:

- Covered entity/system, accountable incident contact, escalation rota, backup contact, and approved reporting channel.
- Detection, occurrence, and awareness timestamps; trusted time synchronization and evidence of clock health.
- Event sources (application, identity, endpoint, network, cloud, database, queue, and third parties), collection owner, field dictionary, integrity controls, and gaps.
- Triage/classification decision, rationale, decision-maker, escalation timestamps, report submission record, recipient acknowledgment, updates, and closure evidence.
- Log retention location and region, access controls, retrieval/export procedure, retention/deletion schedule, preservation/legal hold, and recovery test.
- Exercise evidence for incident notification/escalation, log retrieval, evidence integrity, and handoff across fiduciary/processor and provider/customer roles.

The product's current six-hour incident timer and 180-day endpoint lifecycle-event expiry are implementation metadata/policies only. They are not a statutory reportability determination, a complete log-retention system, or evidence that a specific statutory deadline or log-retention duty has been met.

## Repository evidence and blocking gaps

- Relevant current models are in `prisma/schema.prisma`: `User`, `Session`, `Organization`, `ConsentPurpose`, `PrivacyNotice`, `ConsentArtifact`, `ConsentAudit`, `EndpointDevice`, `EndpointTelemetryEvent`, `EndpointIncident`, `ContainmentCommand`, `ContainmentAuditRecord`, `WebhookEndpoint`, `WebhookDelivery`, and `AuditEvent`.
- The existing endpoint telemetry is deliberately restricted to agent lifecycle events and expires based on server receipt time. It does not collect host OS or complete infrastructure/security logs.
- The incident workflow is manually initiated and its six-hour timer is internal readiness metadata.
- The repository does not establish the hosting region, infrastructure audit sources, legal entity roles, processor contracts, statutory reporting configuration, or production retention controls.
- No assertion in this inventory constitutes legal advice, legal approval, certification, or proof of compliance.
